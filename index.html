Calsifer Twitch Overlay

We as a team got Desca as our client and she wanted a game for her chat to play while nothing interesting is happening during her stream. The key points are: to not take away too much of the stream, to be tailor made for Desca and to be something related to her beloved Calsifer.

After a lot of brainstorming we came up with tamagothci style game where Casifer is the chat's pet. We had a meeting and she liked the idea.

Before doing anything we first had to make the concept of the game idea. We basically sat down and desided to first do applied research. I did Desca's current audience. Then with the outcome we decided to do a tamagothci game and Dylan used the research and ideas and did a concept document. You can see it bellow:



We split up into two teams: the artists and the coders. Later bacame the artists, the figma maniacs and the coder.

The architechture is the following:



Both the gui and overlay are hosted on a server running on localhost 3000. In order to start that server Desca will have to open a terminal inside the folder and type "npm install" to install all the dependancies once and then each time she wants to start the game she will have to open a terminal inside the server folder and type "node server". Then she will have to put the browser overlay inside obs with url "http://localhost:3000/overlay/index.html" and to access the gui she will have to go to "http://localhost:3000/gui/index.html" inside her own browser.

The server communicates to the gyi and overlay with a socket. The actuall commands comming from the chat are handled by streamer.bot where she can connect and modify how the viewer point redeems are handled. All of this is explained in the user guide.

The code is split into front and back. The front is on html and css and the back is in JavaScript.

Jira Board

In order to have a efficient workflow we decided to use the Agile Scrum framework becuase it matched our unpredictable client Desca (she had a period where she basically disappeared). Since my team was close to entering the profecional world of ICT where Jira is the main planning tool I decided it was best for them to use Jira. They were used to using Trello so they weren't very entusiastic about it.

We did a weel of fortune where we decided who was going to be the scrum master and project owner. Malou got the project owner role and I got scrum master.

First I made a Jira board and added everyone to it and started making tasks. Because it is my first time being a scrum master I did't really know how to organise stuff but I got some help from Paul where he explained that tasks must be smaller. For expample the task should not be named "Finish poster" but it should be split into: "research poster definition", "gain inspiration", "conceptualize", etc. The tasks have to be split like that and they should be updated all the time so I can know what everyone is doing at the time. In order to plan the tasks I have to also communicate a lot with my team so I can gain an idea what the general process is like. Also I have to use epics. Here is a picture of the Jira board and backlog:





This helped me and the team keep track of the tasks that we had to do and in what time we had to finish them.

After another talk with a coach (I'm ashamed that I forgot with who it was but I think it was with Paul again) I realised that I have no defenition of done written under each task. This is necessary everyone to know what the final product of the task should be. Since I'm not that experienced in designing I left my team write their own definition of done under their tasks with me only reviewing it. Here is an example:
